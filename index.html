Details

These notes explain how the figures were built and where to read further. All numbers in this report are example data created for the dashboard.

How to read the figures

  • Net revenue is sales after discounts and returns, before tax.
  • Gross margin is net revenue minus product cost and inbound freight, divided by net revenue.
  • NPS is the share of promoters minus the share of detractors from the post-purchase survey. Scores use responses collected in the quarter, with a minimum of 200 replies per region.
  • Year-on-year comparisons use the same calendar dates in 2025. Store openings after 1 July 2025 are included in both periods once they have traded for a full month.

What sits behind the South gap

The South region grew, but more slowly than the rest of the group. Three stores — Graz Süd, Klagenfurt Zentrum, and Villach West — account for about two thirds of the region’s NPS gap. Common issues in survey comments were queue time on Saturdays and missing fresh produce after 16:00.

Household declined because a private-label promotion ended in June and was not replaced. Beverages grew after the summer cooler range was extended online in July.

Conclusion

Keep the growth mix that is already working, and close the two leaks: freight cost and the South service gap.

Recommended actions and next steps

  1. Reset freight before the November peak. Commercial and supply chain should choose a Q4 rate by 24 October, with a target of recovering at least 0.4 percentage points of margin.
  2. Put the three South stores on a six-week recovery plan. Add Saturday checkout cover and a 15:00 fresh-produce refill. Review NPS weekly with the regional manager.
  3. Point the November promotion at beverages and fresh produce. Those categories are already growing and do not need a deep discount to move.
  4. Replace the lapsed household offer. Agree a smaller private-label bundle by 31 October so the category does not stay negative into Q4.
  5. Report back on 6 November with updated margin, South NPS, and whether the promotion plan is live.
